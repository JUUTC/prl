# Lore

Narrative is **light**: lore books, item flavour text, NPC rumours and quest text. There is no branching dialogue.

## Malgareth the Undying

Malgareth was once the realm's greatest champion, the *Shield of the Realm*, and the sworn knight of **Kaedric**, god of war and honour.

When an invading host threatened the realm, Kaedric demanded a price for victory: Malgareth had to sacrifice his sworn companions, the brothers- and sisters-in-arms who followed him. Malgareth refused. Kaedric withdrew his blessing in the middle of the battle and Malgareth's army was slaughtered.

Dying on the field, Malgareth turned to forbidden necromancy so he could keep fighting. He won the battle but lost himself. He rose as a lich, raised his fallen companions as an undying legion, and declared himself **Lich-Emperor**. His grief and rage seep into the land as the **undead blight**.

Kaedric's church rewrote the story: in the official version Malgareth betrayed his god and the realm. Few people know the truth.

## The blight

- The blight spreads slowly from the final dungeon over game time. The rate scales with difficulty and NG+ tier, and resetting the final dungeon also grows it (see [endgame.md](endgame.md)).
- Blighted tiles raise undead encounter rates and area level. They can besiege player houses (see [housing-and-logistics.md](housing-and-logistics.md)) and eventually threaten outlying villages.
- Cleansing shrines, quests and Restoration/Abjuration rituals push the blight back.
- Malgareth's **phylactery** is hidden in another dungeon, revealed through the main quest. If it is not destroyed, Malgareth reforms once on a deeper *Throne of Bone* floor after his first defeat.

## Kaedric's secret

Kaedric remains a **worshipable** god for Clerics and Paladins, but his history carries a twist.

- A **secret quest line**, seeded per world, scatters fragments of the truth in lore books, the ruins of the old battlefield, ghosts of Malgareth's companions, and a hidden chapel.
- When the truth is revealed:
  - **Kaedric's followers** must choose. They can *Keep Faith* (piety intact; boon upgraded to **Unbroken Oath**) or *Renounce* (switch patron with no wrath penalty and gain the **Oathbreaker** perk).
  - **Anyone** carrying the *Testament of the Companions* can present it during the final fight, which weakens Malgareth by skipping one phase, and unlocks an alternate victory text.
- A meta-progression feat is tied to uncovering the truth (see [meta-progression.md](meta-progression.md)).
