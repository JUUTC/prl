# Lore

Narrative is **light**: lore books, item flavour text, NPC rumours and quest text. There is no branching dialogue.

## The Realm of Vhalor

The realm is called **Vhalor**. The name is older than the written records; the road-singers use it for any land that keeps its roads.

Before the war, Vhalor was a patchwork of town-holds with no single capital, held together by three things: the shared worship of the Six, the guilds of the roads, and the old memory of dangers older than the written records. The long peace was called the **Quiet Century**.

The **Order of the Shield** was a fellowship of champion-knights sworn to the temples of Kaedric, raised after older wars to guard the realm's marches. Its highest title, *Shield of the Realm*, passed to the champion the temples trusted most with the realm's defence.

## The Khar'zul War

**Khar'zul** the Warlord raised his host in the eastern wastes — orcish and ogre warbands, giant raiders, and warlocks who spoke to old demons — and marched on Vhalor for the **Stone of Founding**, the relic from which the realm's roads and temples were raised.

The war lasted three years. The town-holds fell one by one, and Vhalor paid for every month in burned fields and empty hearths. In the end the Order of the Shield chose its last stand at the **Field of Broken Oaths**, between the last standing towns. What happened there — the price Kaedric demanded, the refusal, what rose from the dead — is told in the [Malgareth](#malgareth-the-undying) section below.

Khar'zul died at the Field of Broken Oaths, and his name is a curse in the eastern marches. After the war, the Order of the Shield was disbanded, and its banners were taken in by Kaedric's temples.

The churches of the Six rewrote the ending (see the [Malgareth](#malgareth-the-undying) section), but the other five gods did not abandon their worshippers. Solenne's light still burns in the healed towns; Morrow's wardens still walk the blighted roads; Verdana's green retreats season by season; Thalos's archives still grow; Iridel's roads are still kept. No god speaks of what happened at the Field of Broken Oaths.

## Malgareth the Undying

Malgareth was once the realm's greatest champion, the *Shield of the Realm*, and the sworn knight of **Kaedric**, god of war and honour.

When an invading host threatened the realm, Kaedric demanded a price for victory: Malgareth had to sacrifice his sworn companions, the brothers- and sisters-in-arms who followed him. Malgareth refused. Kaedric withdrew his blessing in the middle of the battle and Malgareth's army was slaughtered.

Dying on the field, Malgareth turned to forbidden necromancy so he could keep fighting. He won the battle but lost himself. He rose as a lich, raised his fallen companions as an undying legion, and declared himself **Lich-Emperor**. His grief and rage seep into the land as the **undead blight**.

Kaedric's church rewrote the story: in the official version Malgareth betrayed his god and the realm. Few people know the truth.

## The blight

- The blight spreads slowly from the final dungeon — **The Ashspire**, in the blighted heartland of **the Ashlands** — over game time. The rate scales with difficulty and NG+ tier, and resetting the final dungeon also grows it (see [endgame.md](endgame.md)).
- Blighted tiles raise undead encounter rates and area level. They can besiege player houses (see [housing-and-logistics.md](housing-and-logistics.md)) and eventually threaten outlying villages.
- Cleansing shrines, quests and Restoration/Abjuration rituals push the blight back.
- Malgareth's **phylactery** is hidden in another dungeon, revealed through the main quest. If it is not destroyed, Malgareth reforms once on a deeper *Throne of Bone* floor after his first defeat.

## Kaedric's secret

Kaedric remains a **worshipable** god for Clerics and Paladins, but his history carries a twist.

- A **secret quest line**, seeded per world, scatters fragments of the truth in lore books, the ruins of the old battlefield, ghosts of Malgareth's companions, and a hidden chapel.
- When the truth is revealed:
  - **Kaedric's followers** must choose. They can *Keep Faith* (piety intact; boon upgraded to **Unbroken Oath**) or *Renounce* (switch patron with no wrath penalty and gain the **Oathbreaker** perk).
  - **Anyone** carrying the *Testament of the Companions* can present it during the final fight, which weakens Malgareth by skipping one phase, and unlocks an alternate victory text.
- A meta-progression feat is tied to uncovering the truth (see [meta-progression.md](meta-progression.md)).

## The Pantheon

The Six are not at war with one another; each is tested, in its own way, by the blight. Domains, boons and taboos are gameplay — see [races-and-classes.md](races-and-classes.md#pantheon-clerics--paladins).

- **Solenne, the Dawnfather** — sun, healing. Patient and forgiving; the face of mercy. His church is the one that condemns Malgareth, for the dawn forgives the living and will not look on the risen dead. *"The dawn forgives, but it does not forget."*
- **Verdana, Mother of the Green** — nature, beasts. Quiet and old; speaks through herds and seasons. Keeps no grudge, but the smell of smoke stays with her longest. *"The forest does not curse; it withdraws."*
- **Thalos, the Starkeeper** — knowledge, magic. A patient archivist who keeps oaths to the written word. Neither for nor against the lich — only against forgetting. *"Every name deserves a page, even one that lies."*
- **Morrow, the Grey Warden** — death's passage, anti-undead. Solemn, impartial, weary of carrying the dead. The blight is a wound he will not close. *"What is borrowed from the dead is paid to the living."*
- **Iridel, the Wayfarer** — luck, travel. Practical, light of tongue; keeps every deal but forgets every face. The roads are Iridel's. *"A road is a promise made with both feet."*

Kaedric, god of war and honour, has been silent since the field. His temples are the most powerful in the realm, and his silence is the crux of the [secret quest line](#kaedrics-secret).
